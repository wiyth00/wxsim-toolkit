# Corn Belt Progressive Derecho: Tuesday, July 13, 2027 (simulated)

A textbook high-end progressive derecho and severe wind event across the Upper Midwest and Corn Belt. A fast, quasi-zonal 55–70 kt midlevel jet atop an extreme high-summer instability reservoir (MLCAPE 4500–5500 J/kg, dewpoints in the upper 70s to near 80°F, and DCAPE > 1600 J/kg) drives a long-track, accelerating bow echo system from eastern Iowa across the Chicago metropolitan area, northern Indiana, and northwest Ohio.

## Key features
- **Aloft:** Fast quasi-zonal flow with an embedded 500 mb jet streak (60–75 kt) propagating along the northern periphery of a prominent subtropical heat dome centered over the southern Plains and lower Mississippi Valley.
- **Surface:** A stationary frontal boundary and antecedent outflow reinforced by morning elevated convection is draped from east-central Iowa across northern Illinois (near the I-88/I-80 corridor) into northern Indiana and northern Ohio.
- **Thermodynamic reservoir:** Surface temperatures warming into the lower to mid 90s °F over extreme agricultural evapotranspiration pooling dewpoints in the upper 70s to near 80°F (77–81°F Td). MLCAPE of 4500–5500 J/kg with steep midlevel lapse rates (7.8–8.4 °C/km) advecting eastward from the northern High Plains. Precipitable water exceeding 2.0–2.2 inches and extreme downdraft CAPE (DCAPE 1500–1800 J/kg).
- **Kinematics:** 0–6 km bulk shear of 45–60 kt oriented essentially parallel to the surface boundary, favoring rapid upscale growth into an intense, accelerating quasi-linear convective system (QLCS) with prominent bowing segments, rear-inflow jet (RIJ) descent, and bookend vortices.
- **Hazards:** Widespread destructive straight-line winds (80–105+ mph swaths), embedded QLCS tornadoes from line-apex and bookend mesovortices, and isolated very large hail during early semi-discrete initiation in Iowa.

## 1630Z Day 1 Outlook (High Risk)
- **Area:** High Risk from east-central Iowa (Cedar Rapids, Iowa City, Quad Cities) across northern Illinois (Rockford, DeKalb, Aurora, Chicago metro) into northern Indiana (Gary, South Bend, Fort Wayne) and extreme northwest Ohio (Toledo vicinity).
- **Wind:** 60% with Intensity 2/3 (85–100+ mph gusts) along the core derecho corridor, embedded within an extensive 45% / 30% / 15% / 5% wind envelope.
- **Tornado:** 10% with Intensity 1 (EF2 possible) for rapid spin-up mesovortices along the leading gust front and bookend structures.
- **Hail:** 30% across eastern Iowa, diminishing eastward as storm mode turns predominantly linear and cold-pool driven.
