* Tornado Watch for portions of
  East-Central Alabama
  North and Central Georgia
  Far Southwest North Carolina
  Upstate South Carolina

* Effective this Saturday afternoon and evening from 250 PM until 1100 PM EDT.

...THIS IS A PARTICULARLY DANGEROUS SITUATION...

* Primary threats include...
  Several tornadoes likely with a few intense tornadoes possible
  Widespread large hail and several very large hail events to 4.5 inches in diameter likely
  Scattered damaging winds likely with isolated significant gusts to 80 mph possible

SUMMARY...A rare and dangerous severe weather event for the region is unfolding. Discrete supercells are developing along a sharp dryline in west Georgia and will spread east-northeast across the watch area through the evening. Giant hail up to 4.5 inches in diameter is expected with the most intense storms, and hail may fall well away from the heavier rain. Tornado potential will increase markedly late this afternoon and evening. Supercells will reach a corridor of richer low-level moisture and strong low-level shear along a stalled wedge front across northeast Georgia and the Upstate, where intense tornadoes are possible.

The tornado watch area is approximately along and 70 statute miles north and south of a line from 20 miles south southwest of Anniston AL to 20 miles east northeast of Greenville SC. For a complete depiction of the watch see the associated watch outline update (WOUS64 KWNS WOU4).

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

REMEMBER...A Tornado Watch means conditions are favorable for tornadoes and severe thunderstorms in and close to the watch area. Persons in these areas should be on the lookout for threatening weather conditions and listen for later statements and possible warnings.
