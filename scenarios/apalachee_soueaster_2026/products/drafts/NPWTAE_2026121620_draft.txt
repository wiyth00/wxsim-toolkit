* WHERE...The Florida Big Bend from the Apalachicola River east, and south-central Georgia.

* WHEN...From 7 PM EST this evening to 7 PM EST Friday. The most destructive winds are expected from 11 PM tonight through 4 AM Thursday near the coast, and from 5 AM to 9 AM Thursday from Moultrie and Quitman to Valdosta.

* IMPACTS...Destructive winds. Widespread damage to roofs, siding, and windows. Many large trees will be snapped or uprooted, blocking most roads. * ADDITIONAL DETAILS...This is a once-in-a-generation wind event for the Big Bend and south Georgia. Expect damage comparable to a strong Category 1 or a Category 2 hurricane at landfall, spread over a much larger area. Extreme Wind Warnings may be issued near the core of the low as it comes ashore.

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

Treat this like a landfalling hurricane. Finish preparations before the squall line arrives this evening. Overnight, stay in an interior room on the lowest floor of a sturdy building, away from windows. If you live in a mobile home, leave for a sturdier shelter this afternoon.

There may be a lull of 30 to 60 minutes as the center passes near Tallahassee and Crawfordville early Thursday. Stay inside. The winds will return suddenly from the opposite direction.
